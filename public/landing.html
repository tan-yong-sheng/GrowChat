Three things no other self-hosted chat app gets right.

3-Scope RBAC

Read, Manage, and Manage Access permissions per resource. Admin, Member, and Viewer roles out of the box. No more shared passwords or wild-west API keys.

Multi-Provider LLM

Bring any OpenAI-compatible API — OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Ollama, or your own fine-tuned endpoint. Switch models per conversation. Your keys, your data.
